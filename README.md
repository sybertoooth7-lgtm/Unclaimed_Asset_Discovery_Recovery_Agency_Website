# Unclaimed Asset Discovery & Recovery Agency — starter website

This is a static website starter for a Kenya-focused asset discovery and claim-support business.

Files:
- index.html
- styles.css
- script.js

Before going live:
1. Replace the placeholder email address with your real business email.
2. Connect the enquiry form to a secure backend/CRM.
3. Add a real privacy policy and terms of service.
4. Have the business model, client agreement, fees, data-processing practices and any representation/agency arrangements reviewed by a Kenyan qualified professional.
5. Do not collect passwords, full bank credentials or unnecessary identity documents through a basic web form.
6. Keep claims about UFAA current and clearly distinguish the agency from UFAA.

The website currently links to UFAA's official site and current claiming instructions for verification.
