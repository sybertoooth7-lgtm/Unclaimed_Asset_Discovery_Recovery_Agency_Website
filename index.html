WHY THIS MATTERS

Unclaimed doesn't mean ownerless.

Assets can become unclaimed because of relocation, forgotten accounts, poor records, death or other circumstances. UFAA says examples include dormant bank accounts, unclaimed dividends and shares, utility deposits, retirement benefits and deposits from collapsed institutions.

Our job is not to take ownership. Our job is to help the person with the legitimate interest understand the path to recovery.

Important compliance principle

For an agent acting for a living owner, UFAA's current instructions include an Agent for Owner claim (Form 4D), an indemnity agreement, identification/KRA PIN requirements and a registered Power of Attorney.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Are you UFAA?

No. UADR is an independent private service concept. UFAA is the Kenyan state corporation responsible for receiving, safeguarding and reuniting unclaimed financial assets with rightful owners.

Can you guarantee that I will recover money?

No. A discovery is not proof of ownership and recovery depends on verification, documentation, legal entitlement and the decision of the relevant institution.

Can you claim someone else's money?

Not simply because you found it. Claims must be made by the rightful owner or an appropriately authorized person. For deceased estates and other situations, the required legal documentation can differ.

Do you charge before finding an asset?

Our final commercial model should be published only after the agency's legal, consumer-protection, privacy and tax requirements have been reviewed. Do not promise a percentage or fee on this site until the business model is legally cleared.
